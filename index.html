System readiness

  • Offline browser storage: available on this device after the page has loaded.
  • Branch performance reports: daily, weekly, monthly and yearly sales, collections and balances.
  • Stock inventory: separate branch quantities, receipts, material usage and movement history; metres tracked without purchase costs.
  • Invoices and delivery notes: accepted quotations can be converted to linked sales, with downloadable PDF documents carrying Raysis business details.
  • Cross-device cloud synchronization: connected through Supabase when signed in online.
  • Secure email/password authentication and branch permissions: enforced by Supabase Auth and Row Level Security.
  • Keep regular JSON backups; cloud database backups depend on your Supabase plan.
